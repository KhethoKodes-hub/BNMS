"As black women I saw potential within our community that need to be empowered by companies that has the same potential with passion."
— Boniswa Oor, Director

BNMS OOR PRO (PTY) LTD is a black women owned company, incorporated on 16 December 2021. Based in Gamagara Municipality, Northern Cape, our mission is creating sustainable employment and delivering professional engineering and construction services to mining, municipal and private clients.
