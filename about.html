BNMS OOR PRO (PTY) LTD is a black women owned company, incorporated on Friday, 16 December 2021 with registration no. 2021/151894/07. Based in Gamagara Municipality — Kathu / Dibeng, Northern Cape.

The aim of starting this business is to create jobs and reduce unemployment within our community. We bring sustainable solutions through integrated maintenance and services — giving every customer the quality they deserve.
